01 / About

A youth office that actually listens to youth.

The Yei River County Youth Association (YRC-YA) is an independent, youth-led initiative that is non-political, non-religious and non-profit making. We exist to mobilize, unite, empower and advocate for the rights of young people and other youth groups across Yei River County.

Our work is built around advocacy, empowerment through youth engagement and impact forums, and amplifying local voices of young people. We do this to improve and advance social behaviour change and sustainable development for the young people of Yei River County in Central Equatoria State.

We do not speak for youth. We work alongside them — boda-boda riders, persons with disabilities, faith-based groups, music and drama groups, women and girls, students and out-of-school youth. Our association is open to any young person in Yei who wants to be part of something. No fees, no politics, no hidden conditions. Just work that matters.

Youth voices. Youth action. That is the whole point.

02 / Vision, Mission & Values

What we are building, and how we build it.

Everything YRC-YA does is guided by a clear vision, a practical mission and four values that keep us honest.

Vision

To raise global youth leaders, unify and empower them to become the agents of world social, political and economic transformation.

Mission

Empowering youth without discrimination on gender or any other affiliations through income-generating initiatives and formal education with basic teaching or vocational skills.

Core values

Four values. Every decision.

Peace

We prioritize conflict resolution and long-term stability in everything we do.

Inclusivity

We ensure all community members, including marginalized groups, are included in the recovery process.

Resilience & sustainability

We commit to environmental stewardship and build systems and structures that can withstand future challenges and crises.

Collaboration

We work in partnership with local, national and international stakeholders to achieve common goals.

03 / What we do

Six areas of work, one goal for Yei.

Everything we run fits into one of these areas. If it does not help young people in Yei, we do not do it.

Field work
01

Advocacy & rights

Speaking up on issues that matter to youth and amplifying local voices so young people are part of the decisions that affect them.

02

Empowerment & income

Supporting income-generating initiatives that help young people earn, save and stand on their own.

03

Education & vocational skills

Formal education support with basic teaching, plus practical vocational skills that lead to work and income.

04

Youth engagement & impact forums

Bringing young people together in forums where their voices are heard, recorded and acted on.

05

Social behaviour change

Awareness drives and dialogue that improve and advance social behaviour change for sustainable development.

06

Unity & mobilization

Mobilizing and uniting youth groups across the county so young people speak and act with one strong voice.

If you want to be part of any of these areas, our door is open.

Who we serve

Every youth group has a seat at this table.

YRC-YA is not one group. It is a home for all the youth groups that make Yei what it is — and no young person is left out because of gender, ability, faith or background.

Open to all

Boda-boda group

The riders who keep Yei moving. We organize, train and give them a real voice in county decisions.

Persons with disabilities

Ensuring no young person is left out of opportunities, programs or decisions because of ability.

Faith-based groups

Working hand in hand with youth fellowships and faith communities across the county — non-religious in our work, inclusive in our reach.

Music & drama groups

Creative youth using art, music and performance to speak truth, build peace and mobilize their communities.

Women & girls

Supporting young women to lead, learn and earn — without discrimination on gender or any other affiliation.

Students & out-of-school youth

Reaching both those in school and those who need a second chance through formal education or vocational skills.
